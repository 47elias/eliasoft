What does SchoolDesk help a school manage?+

SchoolDesk brings key school tasks such as student information, attendance, timetables, fees and communication into one system. We can discuss which features fit your school.

Can you build software for a different industry?+

Yes. Eliasoft Technologies Inc. develops custom software and web applications around the needs and workflows of each client.

How do we get started?+

Share a little about your goals using the inquiry form. We’ll use that as a starting point for a conversation about scope and next steps.

Is the inquiry form a commitment?+

No. It creates an email draft for you to review and send. It doesn’t submit an order or sign you up for anything.
